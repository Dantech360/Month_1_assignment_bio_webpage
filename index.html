About Me

My Biography

My name is Daniel Bolaji, and I am an aspiring software engineer currently learning AI-Powered Full-Stack Engineering at AltSchool Africa.

My background is rooted in electrical engineering and renewable energy, where I have gained practical experience working with electrical systems, solar power installations, inverters, batteries, maintenance, electrical switchboard design and construction, and technical problem-solving.

My experience in engineering has taught me to approach problems logically, understand how different systems work together, and look for practical solutions. Over time, my interest has expanded beyond physical electrical systems into the world of software and digital technology.

This transition into software engineering is an opportunity for me to combine my existing engineering mindset with modern technologies such as software development, artificial intelligence, web technologies, and automation.

I am particularly interested in how technology can solve real-world problems and how software can integrate with other areas of engineering.

Why AltSchool Africa

  1. I joined AltSchool Africa because I wanted a structured environment where I could develop practical software engineering skills.

  2. Learning technology independently can provide a lot of information, but I believe structured learning, projects, deadlines, collaboration, and interaction with other learners can provide a stronger foundation.

  3. I also wanted an environment that would challenge me to move from simply understanding concepts to actually building things with those concepts.

  4. For me, AltSchool is therefore not just about learning how to code. It is about developing the ability to think like an engineer, solve problems, collaborate with others, and continuously learn.

  5. i will graduate into an operational community, which will help foster continuous growth and relationship building.

My Goals

  • Technical Goals

My first goal is to develop a strong foundation in software engineering and become comfortable building full-stack applications.

I want to understand both the frontend and backend sides of web development and how they communicate with one another.

I also want to develop a strong understanding of artificial intelligence and learn how AI can be integrated into practical software applications.
